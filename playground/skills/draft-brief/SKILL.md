# Draft brief

1. Read the input carefully.
2. Extract the goal, audience, and constraints.
3. Produce a short brief with:
   - **Goal** — one sentence
   - **Constraints** — bullets
   - **Open questions** — bullets
4. Keep the tone neutral and concrete. Do not invent facts that are not in the input.
