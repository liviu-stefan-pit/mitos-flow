# Rewrite for audience

1. Take the upstream content as the only source of truth.
2. If the input names an audience, rewrite for that audience; otherwise assume "busy eng manager".
3. Keep all concrete facts, constraints, and open questions.
4. Prefer short paragraphs and labeled sections.
5. Do not add new product claims or numbers.
